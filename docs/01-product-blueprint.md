# Product Blueprint

## Purpose

Data Science Judgment Lab trains and measures applied data judgment:

- what data can support
- what data cannot support
- what risks matter
- how confident a responsible analyst should be

The lab should not be positioned as a data science fundamentals course or a
coding exam. It is a judgment calibration lab.

## Relationship To AI Literacy Lab

AI Literacy Lab asks:

> Can you use and evaluate AI responsibly?

Data Science Judgment Lab asks:

> Can you reason from data responsibly under uncertainty?

The new lab should keep the AI Literacy Lab core:

- durable questions
- scenario-based activities
- productive struggle
- local-first progress
- accessible static publishing
- practical transfer to real work

But the interaction model should be more investigative and case-driven.

## Primary Audience

Initial audience:

- analysts and data scientists improving applied judgment
- students or bootcamp learners moving beyond syntax
- managers who need to evaluate data claims
- AI/product teams making data-informed launch decisions
- instructors who want realistic discussion cases

Secondary audience:

- hiring managers evaluating judgment beyond code tests
- teams doing internal calibration and postmortem practice
- professionals who need evidence literacy but are not full-time data scientists

## Product Promise

The lab helps learners answer:

- Am I good at making data decisions when the answer is uncertain?
- Do I know when I know, and when I should hedge?
- Can I explain tradeoffs, assumptions, and risk like a senior practitioner?
- Do I become more or less calibrated when pressure increases?

## Completion Model

Use a hybrid structure:

- each case stands alone
- cases are grouped into short case sets
- pathways and intake help learners choose a route through the library
- the open case library can grow indefinitely

Current completion:

- Judgment Lab Record: at least 20 of 25 cases reviewed
- Full 25-Case Lab Record: all 25 cases reviewed
- require coverage across every major judgment area
- require at least three reviewed cases from each focused route
- require a decision and confidence checkpoint for each completed case
- generate a browser-local, self-attested Judgment Record

The record should remain a learning artifact and calibration profile, not a
proctored credential or certification.

## Case Families

The 25-case suite is organized into judgment families:

1. Evidence and Metrics
2. Experiments and Causality
3. Models in the Real World
4. Uncertainty and Decisions
5. Data Provenance and Measurement Integrity
6. Causal Designs Beyond the A/B Test
7. Operational Models and AI Risk

Each family should support repeat visits without giving away the case trap.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence is the interface.
   The learner should inspect artifacts, not just read explanations.

2. Confidence is part of the answer.
   A correct answer with poor calibration is meaningfully different from a
   correct, well-calibrated answer.

3. Multimedia must change the decision.
   Video, audio, charts, logs, and memos are evidence, not decoration.

4. Productive uncertainty is the point.
   Many cases should contain ambiguity, partial evidence, or pressure to
   overclaim.

5. The final report should reveal a pattern.
   The learner should leave with a Judgment Record, not just a percentage.
