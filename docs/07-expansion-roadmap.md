# Future Case Library Plan

The current public lab contains 25 cases. Future growth should preserve the
investigation model rather than becoming a flat catalog of examples.

## Current Case Families

The library is organized around judgment families that support return visits:

- Evidence and metrics
- Experiments and causality
- Models in the real world
- Uncertainty and decisions
- Data provenance and measurement integrity
- Causal designs beyond the A/B test
- Operational models and AI risk

The public site should refer to these as case families, focus areas, pathways,
or focused routes. Avoid internal build language such as "expansion pack."

## Completion Shape

Completion should stay calibrated to a growing library:

- Judgment Lab Record: at least 20 of 25 cases reviewed, with coverage across
  major judgment areas and focused routes.
- Full 25-Case Lab Record: every replay reviewed.
- Facilitator review: optional classroom or team discussion outside the static
  site.

Scores should remain calibration signals, not pass/fail gates.

## Future Growth Areas

If the project grows beyond 25 cases, high-value areas include:

- decision analytics under scarcity
- time-series and forecasting under structural change
- privacy-preserving analytics and governance review
- agentic AI evaluation and permissioning
- causal transportability and external validity
- simulation, optimization, and resource allocation

## Authoring Bar

Every new case should include:

- a real decision under pressure
- at least two plausible competing stories
- one vivid but insufficient artifact
- one quiet but decisive artifact
- one operational constraint that changes the recommendation
- an expert replay that explains what evidence mattered and what remained uncertain
